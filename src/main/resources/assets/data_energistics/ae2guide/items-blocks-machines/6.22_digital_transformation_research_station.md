# Digital Transformation Research Station

The station consumes materials and energy from an ME network to unlock technology, upgrade individual items, and enhance players.

<ItemImage id="data_energistics:digital_transformation_research_station" scale="6" />
<Recipe id="data_energistics:data_reassembler/digital_transformation_research_station" />

## Placement and connection

The station occupies two blocks horizontally. Its main block is on the right and the housing extends to its local left. Leave the adjacent space free, connect a powered ME network with an available channel, and use the main block to open the interface.

## Research scopes

| Scope | Progress owner | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main technology | Shared research team | Uses the current team's progress |
| Item upgrades | The individual target item | Insert one target item; the resulting upgrades remain on it |
| Player upgrades | The current player | Saved per player rather than shared with the team |

Item upgrades apply to [Complexified Biochips](5.10_complexified_biochips.md), the [ME Digital Anvil](6.21_me_digital_anvil.md), floating guns, and other supported items. Unlock the portable [Player Terminal](7.7_player_terminal.md) through player upgrades.

## Starting, pausing, and resuming

1. Select a node and review its prerequisites, exclusions, materials, duration, and energy requirements.
2. Prepare materials in the network. The material request function can order missing craftable ingredients.
3. Starting a project collects its ingredients, and running it consumes energy. A station state supports at most **two running projects**.
4. Pausing retains invested materials and progress. Resuming does not charge the same ingredients again.
5. An item upgrade project locks the target item while the project exists, preventing a target swap.

Mutually exclusive research restricts later choices. For example, each floating gun can receive only one form conversion, so choose its role before starting.

## If research stops

Check prerequisites, the target item, network connection, channel, energy, and whether both running positions are occupied. Research ingredients, research power, and the upgraded equipment's operating costs are separate requirements.
