# Goety

| Machine | Target | Admission and dispatch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dark Altar | Dark Altar block | No ritual is active and its item slot is empty; prepare recipe activation items, bases, and a soul cage; use a ritual with a definite item result. |

The adapter creates a task player for supported item crafting, enchanting, and locating rituals. The packaged enchanting route does not charge player experience, but recipe materials, souls, and structures are still required. Summoning and entity-transformation rituals are not item-processing orders.
