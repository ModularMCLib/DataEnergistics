# Astral Sorcery

| Machine | Target | Admission and dispatch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Astral Altar Crafting | Altar block | Prepare recipe pedestals, relays, fluids, and central input; native altar structure and starlight conditions are valid; one round per operation. |
| Infuser | Infuser block | Pattern contains a single item input; surrounding fluid layout matches the recipe and no ritual is active; the native Infuser consumes fluid and advances the craft. |

The altar route claims the relays used by the recipe and follows native completion. Infusion uses native fluid recipes and timing. For Lumen, constellation, and Liquid Starlight transfers, see the [Astral Sorcery supply page](6.18.2.1_astral_sorcery.md).
