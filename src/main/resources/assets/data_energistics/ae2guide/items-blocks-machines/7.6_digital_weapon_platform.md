# Digital Weapon Platform and Floating Guns

The wearable platform deploys installed floating guns, selects targets, and pays operating costs from a quantum-linked ME network. Chip research determines weapon capacity, permission to use converted forms, and additional abilities.

## First deployment

1. Equip the platform in the helmet slot. With Curios installed, its `head` accessory slot also works; the helmet slot takes priority if both contain platforms.
2. Press **K** by default to open the platform, then insert one gun per unlocked weapon slot.
3. Two weapon slots are available initially. Research a [Complexified Biochip](5.10_complexified_biochips.md) and install it in the chip slot to expand capacity or permit converted guns.
4. Build and power an AE2 Quantum Network Bridge. Put one paired singularity in the bridge and carry its partner. Keep the bridge's chunk loaded.
5. Confirm the connected status, enable the desired weapons and target categories, and the guns will deploy and acquire targets automatically.

The platform uses carried Quantum Entangled Singularities directly; Player Terminal research is not required. Credentials in the dedicated singularity accessory slot take priority over ordinary inventory credentials. With several pairs carried, check which network is selected.

## Chip upgrades and gun conversions

There are at most eight weapon positions and one chip slot. Capacity I and II each add two slots. Floating Gun Platform Mode adds another two and permits converted forms.

Place the gun itself in the [Digital Transformation Research Station](6.22_digital_transformation_research_station.md) to choose grenade, medical, plasma, or sniper conversion. **These four conversions are mutually exclusive and belong to the individual gun.** The installed chip must also have Floating Gun Platform Mode to accept it.

A chip cannot be removed while installed guns rely on its capacity or form permission. Remove those guns before replacing the chip.

## Forms, energy, and heat

The table lists base values per gun. Twenty ticks equal one second. Special ammunition costs resources in addition to AE.

| Form | Base effect | Interval | AE per shot | Heat per shot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | Laser, 6 damage | 10 ticks | 240 | 10 |
| Sniper | Long laser, 24 damage | 40 ticks | 1600 | 30 |
| Plasma | 8 impact damage, 2 arc damage | 20 ticks | 800 | 18 |
| Grenade | 14 damage at the center, decreasing with distance; radius 3, no block damage | 30 ticks | 1200 | 25 |
| Medical | Restores 1 health and grants short support effects; also costs 1 Binary | 10 ticks | 240 | 10 |

Each gun tracks heat independently, up to 100. At the limit it stops firing until cooled to zero. Base cooling is 0.5 per tick, increasing to 0.7 with both chip cooling upgrades. Multiple guns draw energy and ammunition independently.

Medical guns support only the owner and allied players, with separate self/team switches. They pause if either AE or Binary is unavailable.

## Ammunition and targets

AE is standard ammunition. An attacking gun can select one special resource effect; click the selected effect again to cancel it.

| Special ammunition | Extra resource per shot |
| --- | --- |
| Binary | 1 |
| Data Flow | 100 |
| Stellar Flux | 100 |
| Echo | 5 |

If special resources run out, attacking guns fall back to standard AE ammunition and resume the selected effect when stock returns. Medical guns always require Binary.

Set hostile, neutral, friendly, player, or other target categories globally or per gun. Hostiles are selected by default. Friendly protection excludes allied players and the owner's tame animals; player attacks also obey server PvP restrictions.

Automatic fire control acquires targets within **160 blocks** of the player. The larger radar range does not extend that targeting radius.

## Default controls

| Action | Binding |
| --- | --- |
| Open platform | K |
| Toggle the target under the crosshair | Tap R |
| Sweep to add target locks | Hold R; also hold Ctrl to remove locks |
| Rally guns at a pointed surface | Alt + left-click, within 160 blocks and loaded chunks |
| Interact with the tactical HUD | J, after chip research |

Change bindings in the controls menu. Shields, flight, prospecting, and other chip abilities must be researched, enabled, and supplied with their own operating costs.

## If a gun does not fire

Check equipment placement, unlocked slots, chip permission for the gun form, quantum bridge status, AE and resource stocks, weapon and attack toggles, target filters, friendly protection, and heat. The interface distinguishes network, resource, searching, cooldown, and overheated states.
