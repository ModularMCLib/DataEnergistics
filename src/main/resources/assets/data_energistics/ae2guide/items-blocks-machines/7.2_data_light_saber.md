# Data Light Saber, Sanctifier and Data Trident

These weapons share one energy store for AE and FE charging. Research energy capacity and abilities in a Modification Station. Light sabers and sanctifiers also support three speed levels. The Cell Workbench no longer installs cards.

## Data Light Saber

> Middle School Lesser Quotes:   
> A fun yet dangerous "little toy" that lets you cosplay as a Jedi?  
> 

<Row><ItemImage id="data_energistics:data_light_saber" scale="4" /><RecipeFor id="data_energistics:data_light_saber" /></Row>

Use toggles four orbiting sabers; research raises this to eight. Each saber consumes 80 AE/s and deals half panel damage, with at most one hit per target per caster each second. The orbit continues while its source weapon changes hands, is dropped, or is stored. It ends when toggled off or depleted, then starts a 45-second cooldown.

Digitized strikes are unlocked through research. Swings no longer emit sword waves. In the inventory, use dye or a Color Applicator on the saber to select any of the original 16 blade colors.

## Data Sanctifier

<Row><ItemImage id="data_energistics:data_sanctifier" scale="4" /><RecipeFor id="data_energistics:data_sanctifier" /></Row>

Hold use for 10 seconds, then release to strike. Early release cancels. Charging consumes 240 AE/s and stops when energy is insufficient. Life Reduction builds to level V: 5% less maximum health per level, up to 25%. It remains for 40 seconds after charging; expiration restores the limit without healing.

A full charge throws the secondary blade into the chest of the nearest unobstructed player or hostile mob in a 120-degree cone within 8 blocks. Players then receive the primary-blade execution and a 20-second spectator interval after respawning. Hostile mobs take half panel damage. With no target, the blade flies forward and remains at its landing point. The first sanctifier player kill grants Strength III, Speed III, Resistance II and Data Overload IV for 20 seconds. Further sanctifier player kills during this period raise each effect one level and extend them by 3 seconds. Each Data Overload level adds 1% attack speed.

The curved sanctifier is held in the right hand while the straight secondary blade rests diagonally at the left hip. Charging lifts the secondary into the left hand; releasing throws it. Approach the blade or its target to recover it automatically. If the target dies, the blade stays at that location. Charging is unavailable until recovery and resumes immediately afterward, with no cooldown. Neither blade uses a scabbard. Ordinary swings use vanilla animations and no longer emit sword waves.

## Data Trident

<Row><ItemImage id="data_energistics:data_trident" scale="4" /></Row>

Use still charges a throw; sneak-use switches repulsion and attraction. After Mob Field research, a flying or landed trident affects unobstructed non-player creatures within 4 blocks for 160 AE/s. It uses energy instead of durability.
