# ME Digital Anvil

The ME Digital Anvil uses energy and experience from the ME network for repairs, renaming, and enchantment combinations. Item research unlocks further enchantment operations.

<ItemImage id="data_energistics:me_digital_anvil" scale="6" />
<Recipe id="data_energistics:data_reassembler/me_digital_anvil" />

## Connection and use

1. Connect a powered ME network to the anvil's **bottom face** and provide a channel. Other faces do not accept ME cable connections.
2. Put the target item in the left slot and repair materials, another matching item, or an enchanted book in the right slot. Enter a new name if needed.
3. Review the result and quoted cost. Ensure the network contains enough AE and experience resources, then take the result.

Payment is collected when the result is taken. Experience is paid from network storage; the player's carried experience levels do not replace that resource. A visible preview does not mean payment has already occurred.

## Item research

Place the anvil item in the [Digital Transformation Research Station](6.22_digital_transformation_research_station.md) and select item upgrades. Research belongs to that individual anvil and remains with it when dismantled.

| Research | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed Penalty | Uses a fixed penalty instead of accumulating prior-work penalties |
| Unrestricted Types | Allows enchantments that would normally be incompatible or inapplicable |
| Unrestricted Levels | Allows levels beyond vanilla limits and extra level adjustments, up to level 255 |
| Copy Enchantments | With an enchanted item on the left and a plain book on the right, produces an enchanted book while retaining the original item |

Level adjustment buttons change the value by one normally, ten with Shift, or one hundred with Ctrl. Enhanced operations may also require Data Flow, Stellar Flux, or Echo. Check the current operation's quote for the exact cost.

## If the result cannot be taken

Check the bottom connection, channel, network power, experience, and other required resources. Vanilla enchantment type and level limits still apply until their corresponding research is complete.

The anvil workspace in the portable [Player Terminal](7.7_player_terminal.md) uses separate player research. Upgrading this block does not unlock the portable workspace's upgrades.
