# Digital Weapon Platform and Floating Guns

The wearable platform deploys installed floating guns, selects targets, and pays operating costs from a quantum-linked ME network. Chip research determines weapon capacity, permission to use converted forms, and additional abilities.

## Crafting

Both items are crafted in the [Data Reassembler](6.3_data_reassembler.md). Each recipe produces one item before output upgrades.

### Digital Weapon Platform

<Recipe id="data_energistics:data_reassembler/digital_weapon_platform" />

The recipe requires **6 B of Ender** (6,000 mB) and **14,000 Data Flow**.

### Digital Floating Gun

<Recipe id="data_energistics:data_reassembler/digital_floating_gun" />

The recipe uses the cable-mounted ME Beam Former and requires **2 B of Data Corrosion Liquid** (2,000 mB) and **3,000 Data Flow**.

## First deployment

1. Equip the platform in the helmet slot. With Curios installed, its `head` accessory slot also works; the helmet slot takes priority if both contain platforms.
2. Press **K** by default to open the platform, then insert one gun per unlocked weapon slot.
3. Two weapon slots are available initially. Research a [Complexified Biochip](5.10_complexified_biochips.md) and install it in the chip slot to expand capacity or permit converted guns.
4. Build and power an AE2 Quantum Network Bridge. Put one paired singularity in the bridge and carry its partner. Keep the bridge's chunk loaded.
5. Confirm the connected status, enable the desired weapons and target categories, and the guns will deploy and acquire targets automatically.

The platform uses carried Quantum Entangled Singularities directly; Player Terminal research is not required. Credentials in the dedicated singularity accessory slot take priority over ordinary inventory credentials. With several pairs carried, check which network is selected.

## Chip upgrades and gun conversions

There are at most eight weapon positions and one chip slot. Capacity I and II each add two slots. Floating Gun Platform Mode adds another two and permits converted forms.

Place the gun itself in the [Digital Transformation Research Station](6.22_digital_transformation_research_station.md) to choose grenade, medical, plasma, or sniper conversion. **These four conversions are mutually exclusive and belong to the individual gun.** The installed chip must also have Floating Gun Platform Mode to accept it.

A chip cannot be removed while installed guns rely on its capacity or form permission. Remove those guns before replacing the chip.

## Forms, energy, and heat

The table lists base values per gun. Twenty ticks equal one second. Special ammunition costs resources in addition to AE.

| Form | Base effect | Interval | AE per shot | Heat per shot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | Laser, 240 damage | 10 ticks | 240 | 10 |
| Sniper | Long laser, 1600 damage | 40 ticks | 1600 | 30 |
| Plasma | 50 impact damage, 750 damage per arc hit | 20 ticks | 800 | 18 |
| Grenade | 480 damage at the center, decreasing with distance; radius 3, no block damage | 30 ticks | 480 | 15 |
| Medical | Restores 1% of the target's maximum health, at least 4 health, and grants short support effects; also costs 1 Binary | 10 ticks | 240 | 13 |

Each gun tracks heat independently, up to 100. At the limit it stops firing until cooled to zero. Base cooling is 0.5 per tick, increasing to 0.7 with both chip cooling upgrades. Multiple guns draw energy and ammunition independently.

Medical guns support only the owner and allied players, with separate self/team switches. They pause if either AE or Binary is unavailable.

## Ammunition and targets

AE is standard ammunition. An attacking gun can select one special resource effect; click the selected effect again to cancel it.

| Special ammunition | Extra resource per shot |
| --- | --- |
| Binary | 1 |
| Data Flow | 100 |
| Stellar Flux | 100 |
| Echo | 5 |

If special resources run out, attacking guns fall back to standard AE ammunition and resume the selected effect when stock returns. Medical guns always require Binary.

Set hostile, neutral, friendly, player, or other target categories globally or per gun. Hostiles are selected by default. Friendly protection excludes allied players and the owner's tame animals; player attacks also obey server PvP restrictions.

Automatic fire control acquires targets within **160 blocks** of the player. The larger radar range does not extend that targeting radius.

## Layered vision

Low-light vision, thermal imaging, sonar and night vision have independent switches in the platform's support settings. Each active capability costs **32 AE/t** and requires its research on the installed chip plus an online quantum network.

| Capability | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Low-light vision | Green phosphor image intensification that amplifies existing light, with gain, auto brightness, noise and short-range IR illumination controls |
| Thermal imaging | Maps the entire visible scene to surface temperatures without requiring visible light, respecting walls; click the palette button to cycle White Hot, False Color and Heat Overlay |
| Sonar | Emits an expanding echo every 4 seconds from the player's position, briefly revealing continuous terrain surfaces and entity silhouettes through walls within a fixed **48-block spherical radius** |
| Night vision | Full-strength brightness enhancement; research the three basic capabilities on the same chip first |

Thermal imaging alone includes ordinary terrain, buildings and objects in the thermal image. Visible colors and ordinary lighting do not determine temperature: living creatures, burning entities, fire, lava and lit equipment are hotter, while water, snow and ice are colder. With low-light vision, the selected thermal style overlays heat on the green intensified image; with full night vision, it retains that enhanced scene as the background.

Sonar can run alongside both: each wave expands from its origin, briefly revealing caves, building surfaces and entity shapes behind walls. Adjacent coplanar blocks share a continuous surface without dividing lines; only changes in the surface shape retain contours. Solid interiors do not receive a wireframe around every block. Environmental rendering reads only chunks already loaded by the client.

Enabling low-light vision expands its controls below the switch. Gain and noise use sliders, with mouse-wheel adjustment in **one percentage point** steps. Gain ranges from **50% to 800%**, defaulting to **200%**; auto brightness starts enabled; noise ranges from **0% to 100%**, defaults to **20%**, and can be set to 0. The IR illuminator starts disabled. Enable it for a forward beam reaching visible surfaces within **12 blocks**, revealing nearby surroundings without natural light. All controls are saved with the platform.

Full night vision suspends low-light vision's green intensification, IR illuminator and energy charge, retaining its requested switch and settings. Low-light vision resumes when night vision stops and its own conditions are met. Thermal imaging and sonar remain independent; three effective capabilities cost **96 AE/t** together.

Existing night-vision research is retained, but requires thermal imaging, sonar and low-light research on that chip before it becomes effective again.

## Flight research and mining immunity

Wearing the platform with a chip that has creative-flight research removes airborne, underwater, mining-fatigue and negative mining-speed attribute penalties. Tool harvesting requirements, efficiency and haste bonuses remain effective. Mining immunity works with flight disabled, offline or without power.

Normal and held-sprint base flight speeds are configured separately, defaulting to **5.6** and **24 blocks/s**, both within **5.6–100 blocks/s**. The previous maximum-speed value becomes the sprint setting. Other sources of speed stacking keep their existing behavior.

Basic flight retains inertia. Complete Flight Inertia Cancellation after creative-flight research to unlock its switch. It defaults to enabled, stops movement immediately when movement keys are released, and has no additional ongoing energy cost. Existing chips need the new research; movement protection retains its existing strict movement lock.

## Shields and reboot

Shield absorption and resistance are evaluated before reboot for lethal damage, direct health loss, forced death and lethal removal. A successful defense stops that death before drops or death notifications. A defended lethal reduction of maximum health also restores the previous valid health limit. Reboot retains its recorded bodily state and shared **five-minute cooldown**. **Orbital erasure bypasses both shields and reboot.**

## Disarm protection

Research Disarm Protection after Digital Weapon Platform Research and wear the platform to prevent forced removal of the platform, its installed chip and floating guns. Research costs **400 ticks, 4000 AE/t, 32 Data and 16 Data Processors**. The independent master switch in defense settings defaults to enabled. Extra protection for armor, accessories, the main hand and the off hand defaults to disabled.

Disarm protection works offline without ongoing power, independently of shield charge and reboot cooldown. Voluntary unequipping, dropping, swapping, inventory organization and platform-slot actions remain available, as do normal item consumption and durability breakage. Actual death follows the existing drops and inventory-retention rules.

## Default controls

| Action | Binding |
| --- | --- |
| Open platform | K |
| Toggle the target under the crosshair | Tap R |
| Sweep to add target locks | Hold R; also hold Ctrl to remove locks |
| Rally guns at a pointed surface | Alt + left-click, within 160 blocks and loaded chunks |
| Interact with the tactical HUD | J, after chip research |

Change bindings in the controls menu. Shields, flight, prospecting, and other chip abilities must be researched, enabled, and supplied with their own operating costs.

## If a gun does not fire

Check equipment placement, unlocked slots, chip permission for the gun form, quantum bridge status, AE and resource stocks, weapon and attack toggles, target filters, friendly protection, and heat. The interface distinguishes network, resource, searching, cooldown, and overheated states.
