# Actually Additions

| Machine | Target | Admission and dispatch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Empowerer | Central Empowerer | Four Display Stands are at the same height and three blocks from the center; the center and all stand inventories are empty; auxiliaries are inserted before the main input. |
| Atomic Reconstructor | Reconstructor | Lens matches the recipe, energy is available, the light path is valid, and the first block in front is clear; conversion batches by lens and energy, while dyeing runs item by item. |

The Empowerer requires Display Stands around it. Other Empowerers cannot substitute for them. The Reconstructor faces its laser direction. Keep the provider out of the beam path and remove convertible blocks or item entities from the inspected light path.
