# Mystical Agriculture

This integration supports the Infusion Altar and Awakening Altar. Connect to the central altar. The route uses the real pedestals, essence containers, and native activation logic.

| Machine | Target | Admission and dispatch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Infusion Altar | Central altar | All eight infusion pedestals are present; altar, pedestal slots, and output are empty; peripheral inputs are inserted before the central input. |
| Awakening Altar | Central awakening altar | Four awakening pedestals and four essence containers are present and empty; the pattern declares the required essence types and amounts; the altar is activated and collected round by round. |

Place every peripheral block at the altar's height. Put four at the cardinal positions three blocks from the altar, and four at the diagonals two blocks out along each horizontal axis. For the Awakening Altar, one working layout puts pedestals at the cardinal positions and essence containers at the diagonals.

Connect the provider to the central altar. Leave the essence containers empty for the provider to fill from the pattern. The provider activates both altars directly.
