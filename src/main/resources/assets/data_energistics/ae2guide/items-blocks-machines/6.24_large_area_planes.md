# Large and Extremely Large Planes

Large and Extremely Large Planes extend AE2 Formation and Annihilation Planes into multi-cell devices. Formation planes draw configured resources from the ME network and place blocks, fluids, or items in front of the plane. Annihilation planes harvest blocks, fluids, and dropped items in front of the plane and insert the results into the ME network.

| Plane size | Working area | Working cells |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Large | 3 × 3 × 3 | 27 |
| Extremely Large | 5 × 5 × 5 | 125 |

The plane faces its working area and connects to the ME network from its back. Placing the main plane builds its connected segments automatically. The entire face must fit in replaceable, loaded world space and within the world border. Removing the main plane also removes its connected segments.

Right-click a plane to open its configuration screen. Formation planes provide a 3D preview for selecting each target cell, choosing a resource, and setting it to place a block or continuously drop items. Scroll through the depth layers to configure targets farther away. Annihilation-plane filters apply to the whole working area; leaving the filter empty allows all supported contents. The plane needs an online ME network to operate.
