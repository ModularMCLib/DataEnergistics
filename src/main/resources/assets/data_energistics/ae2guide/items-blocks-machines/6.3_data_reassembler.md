# Data Reassembler
> Junior High-Child Quotes:<br>
> Data Reassembler fragments of information weave a vision of future intelligence and weaving.
>
The Data Reassembler combines items, fluids, and special resources according to recipes. It produces many of the mod's advanced materials and machines.

## Inputs, outputs, and connection

Connect a powered ME network from the back, either side, or the bottom. The front and top do not accept ME connections. Put recipe items, liquids, and special resources such as Binary or Data Flow in their respective input areas.

| Storage area | Input / output slots | Capacity per slot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Items | 9 / 3 | 16384 items |
| Fluids | 2 / 2 | 16384000 mB |
| Special resources | 1 / 1 | 1638400000 units |

Internal energy storage holds **160000 AE**. Special resource slots hold the corresponding real resource rather than ordinary inventory contents; fluids and special resources have separate areas.

Configure the input and output faces for pipes or pattern providers. To push results automatically, enable automatic export and provide a receiving target. These capacities describe machine storage, not the ingredients required for a single operation.

There are **six upgrade slots** in total. Individual card limits do not allow every type to be filled to its limit at once.

| Upgrade | Limit | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saber Energy Card | 2 | No card: 1 parallel operation; one card: 512; two cards: 1024 |
| Speed Card | 4 | Duration = max(1, recipe base duration − card count × 40) ticks |
| Multiplication Card | 1 | Doubles produced items, fluids, and special resources |

A recipe with the default duration of 200 ticks takes 40 ticks with four Speed Cards. Individual recipes may define another base duration. Each parallel operation still consumes a full recipe's ingredients; available inputs and output space limit the actual batch. The Multiplication Card increases output per operation without increasing recipe ingredients.

## Example recipes and troubleshooting

Try [Complexified Biochips](5.10_complexified_biochips.md) or the [Advanced Digital Annihilation Core](5.13_advanced_digital_annihilation_core.md), checking the item, Data Corrosion Liquid, and special resource requirements separately.

- Will not start: check power, ingredient types and amounts, and the fluid and special resource input areas.
- Stops during use: check all output areas, including the extra space needed for multiplied outputs.
- Batch below the parallel limit: provide a full recipe's ingredients for every operation.
- Automatic export does not work: check the export toggle, face configuration, and whether the adjacent target accepts that resource type.
