## Modification Station Upgrades

Place the tool in a Modification Station to research energy capacity, speed, damage, mining speed, Data Flow storage and tool abilities separately. Cards are consumed as research materials. The Cell Workbench no longer installs cards, and old cards do not unlock research.

Energy and speed research each have three levels. Maximum energy is base capacity × (1 + 8 × energy level). The cutting knife starts with 256 bytes of Data Flow storage and gains 512 bytes after research. Other tools unlock their Data Flow storage through a separate research node.

| Tool | Ability research |
| :--- | :--- |
| Crystal Sword | Fully charged attacks consume 20 Data Flow to apply Radix Loss |
| Crystal Axe | Chain felling for 20 Data Flow |
| Crystal Pickaxe | Ore duplication for 20 Data Flow |
| Crystal Hoe | Persistent moist farmland for 20 Data Flow |
| Crystal Shovel | Area mining for 20 Data Flow; sneak-use changes radius |
| Cutting Knife | Anchor selection expands from 9 to 11 chunks |
