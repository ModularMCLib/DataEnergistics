# Draconic Evolution

| Machine | Target | Admission and dispatch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fusion Crafting | Fusion core | Core, output, and discovered injectors are empty; injector tier, count, facing, distance, and line of sight satisfy the recipe; injectors are filled before the core catalyst. |

Injector range and minimum distance use Draconic Evolution configuration. Power the injectors and keep the path to the core unobstructed. Injector items retained by the recipe are recovered after fusion and must be declared in pattern outputs. Use a single-round pattern for non-stackable equipment. Select an output matching mode that handles UUID, module, or energy component changes.
