# Malum

| Route | Target | Admission and dispatch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spirit Infusion | Spirit Altar | Center, soul inventory, and auxiliary inventory are empty; enough nearby pedestals are available; dispatch auxiliaries, souls, and the main input, then collect the result and actual rewards. |
| Spirit Focusing | Spirit Crucible Core | A Spirit Crucible Component is directly above the core; preload the main material and leave the soul inventory empty; native modifiers and material loss remain active. |
| Runic Workbench | Runic Workbench | Supply the recipe's primary and secondary materials; output position is available; collect the recipe result. |

Transfer the Spirit Infusion, Spirit Focusing, or Runeworking recipe from JEI. Spirit Focusing's dedicated main material may be preloaded for that route. Do not preload ordinary consumables. Extra rewards are recorded from actual collection and are not treated as fixed recipe outputs.
