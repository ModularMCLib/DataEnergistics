# Concentrated Silicon Carbon Meteorite Crystal

<ItemImage id="data_energistics:concentrated_silicon_carbon_meteorite_crystal" scale="6" />

This crystal is obtained through the Incineration world interaction. It can be processed into silicon or printed silicon, or burned as fuel.

## Incineration

<GameScene zoom="6" background="transparent">
    <Block id="ae2:mysterious_cube" x="0" y="0" z="0" />
    <Block id="minecraft:fire" x="0" y="1" z="0" />
    <IsometricCamera yaw="205" pitch="25" />
</GameScene>

1. Place AE2's **Mysterious Cube** and light ordinary fire directly on top of it.
2. Drop the ingredients below into the same fire as item entities.
3. Once the ingredients are present, they turn into one crystal. Collect the result.

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| AE2 Sky Stone (`ae2:sky_stone_block`) | 2 |
| Silicon (`c:silicon` tag) | 1 |
| Coal (`minecraft:coals` tag, including charcoal) | 1 |

Use ordinary Sky Stone block items; no packaging is required. **The Mysterious Cube is the block below the fire, and this recipe does not transform it.** JEI and EMI also display this world interaction.

## Processing and fuel

| Use | Base result |
| --- | --- |
| Stonecutter | One crystal → 6 AE2 Silicon |
| Inscriber with a Silicon Press | One crystal → 6 Printed Silicon; the press is retained |
| Furnace fuel | Smelts 40 items per crystal, with a burn time of 8000 ticks |
