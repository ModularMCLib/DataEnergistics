# Neo Vitae

| Machine | Target | Admission and dispatch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ara Vitae | Ara Vitae altar | Supply recipe materials; native machine provides and consumes Essentia Vitae. |
| Hellfire Forge | Hellfire Forge | Prepare recipe inputs; the player manages the dedicated gem slot, which is not inspected by the adapter. |
| Tabula Vitae | Tabula Vitae main block | Prepare recipe inputs; the player manages the dedicated orb slot, while other inspected inventory must be empty. |

Connect the machine body and prepare its native structure and energy conditions. Results and returns are recorded from actual collection. Dedicated gems and orbs are not packaged inputs and should not be added as recipe consumables.
