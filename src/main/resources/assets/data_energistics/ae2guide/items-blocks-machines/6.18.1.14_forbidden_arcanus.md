# Forbidden Arcanus

| Machine | Target | Admission and dispatch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hephaestus Forge | Any Hephaestus Forge tier | Actual forge tier satisfies ritual requirements; central input, pedestal materials, and essence cost match; no ritual is active. |

The provider inserts real materials into the forge. The native ritual consumes essence and processes the recipe. Patterns must include valid center and pedestal ingredients. Native modifiers, souls, and structure conditions still apply.
