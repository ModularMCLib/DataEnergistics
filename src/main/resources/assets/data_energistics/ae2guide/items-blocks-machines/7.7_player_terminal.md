# Player Terminal (Portable Overlay)

The Player Terminal is the small portable interface in the inventory screen. Player research unlocks it, and its interface and settings are saved per player. No separate terminal item needs to be crafted or carried.

## Unlocking and opening

1. Open the [Digital Transformation Research Station](6.22_digital_transformation_research_station.md), select **player upgrades**, and research Player Terminal.
2. The default ingredients are 16 Binary, 8 Data Processors, and 4 Digisidian Memorize Ingots, plus research time and energy.
3. Open your inventory and left-click the Player Terminal entry to expand it. Hold the left mouse button to reposition the entry or panel.

The right-click large-terminal entry is reserved for a future feature; use the small overlay through left-click. Research belongs to the current player and is not granted automatically by a teammate's progress.

## Quantum connection

<ItemImage id="ae2:quantum_entangled_singularity" scale="4" />

Put one of a paired set of Quantum Entangled Singularities in an AE2 Quantum Network Bridge connected to ME. Carry the partner in your inventory or a compatible singularity accessory slot. Keep the bridge's chunk loaded and its network powered. An ordinary unpaired singularity is not a credential.

Dedicated singularity accessory credentials take priority over ordinary inventory credentials. Check the selected network when carrying several pairs. This connection does not use a charged wireless terminal or a wireless access point's range.

Network access costs **22.5 AE/t**, charged only once for the same tick. Storage transfers, anvil operations, and other actions have their own costs. Network operations and automation pause when disconnected or short of power.

## Five workspaces

| Workspace | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Crafting | Nine-slot crafting, with ingredients supplied from ME when connected |
| Processing | Edits processing-pattern inputs, outputs, and quantities; does not replace a processing machine |
| Smithing | Uses normal smithing recipes |
| Stonecutting | Selects a stonecutting recipe and crafts its output |
| Digital Anvil | Repairs, renames, and combines enchantments, paying through the connected network |

Local crafting, smithing, and stonecutting use real ingredients. Processing configuration icons cannot be extracted as real stock. Transfer supported recipes from JEI or EMI and use the workspace's encoding controls to create patterns. The anvil workspace does not encode patterns.

The anvil's four enhancements require separate **Player Terminal branch** research. Research stored on an [ME Digital Anvil](6.21_me_digital_anvil.md) item does not grant portable anvil upgrades.

## Storage and automation

- Search, filter resource types, switch stored/craftable views, sort results, and pin frequently used resources.
- Stock maintenance accepts a resource, lower and upper limits, and a request size. Automatic requests still require network patterns, available crafting CPUs, and ingredients.
- Terminal Magnet is a further player research. Configure attraction, direct ME insertion, and inventory restocking through their switches and filters.
- Closing the panel does not disable enabled background restocking, magnet functions, or stock maintenance. Disable each function when it is no longer wanted.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Check |
| --- | --- |
| No inventory entry | Player Terminal research on the current player |
| Overlay opens but stays offline | Paired carried credential, credential priority, bridge chunk loading, and power |
| Automatic crafting does not finish | Patterns, CPU, ingredients, processing equipment, and network energy |
| Missing anvil enhancements | Player-branch research rather than upgrades on an anvil item |

For the cable part that combines terminal types, see the [Universal Terminal](6.23_universal_terminal.md). For wearable weapons, see the [Digital Weapon Platform and Floating Guns](7.6_digital_weapon_platform.md).
