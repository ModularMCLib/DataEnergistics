# Occultism

| Route | Target | Admission and dispatch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spirit Fire | Spirit Fire block | Only recipes with a definite item result are supported. Each round uses one complete item key. The adapter creates and immediately recovers the input entity to prevent a second native conversion. |
| Crafting Ritual | Central golden sacrificial bowl | Magic circle and recipe conditions are valid, and input bowls are idle; the adapter inserts activation items and materials for the native ritual. |

Keep the native support block below Spirit Fire and do not bind that support as the target. Summoning, weather, and command rituals without a definite item result are not AE processing orders.
