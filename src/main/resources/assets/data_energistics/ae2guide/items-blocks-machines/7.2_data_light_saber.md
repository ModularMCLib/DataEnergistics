# Data Light Saber, Sanctifier and Data Trident

These weapons share one energy store for AE and FE charging. Research energy capacity and abilities in a Modification Station. Light sabers and sanctifiers also support three speed levels. The Cell Workbench no longer installs cards.

## Data Light Saber

> Middle School Lesser Quotes:   
> A fun yet dangerous "little toy" that lets you cosplay as a Jedi?  
> 

<Row><ItemImage id="data_energistics:data_light_saber" scale="4" /><RecipeFor id="data_energistics:data_light_saber" /></Row>

Use toggles four orbiting sabers; research raises each ring to eight. Counter-Rotating Rings adds an upper ring with the same count, doubling 4–8 sabers to 8–16. The lower ring keeps its direction, the upper ring spins in reverse, and each cutting edge follows its own ring. Their tips point outward and their cutting edges face the direction of rotation, forming a horizontal cutting wheel. Each saber consumes 80 AE/s and deals half panel damage, with at most one hit per target per caster each second. The orbit continues while its source weapon changes hands, is dropped, or is stored. It ends when toggled off or depleted, then starts a 45-second cooldown.

Digitized strikes are unlocked through research and stack Radix Redundancy without Stasis. The light saber and sanctifier primary share the same three-slash attack sequence, with each handle gripped in a diagonal ready pose. The sequence restarts at the first slash after a pause. Swings no longer emit sword waves. In the inventory, use dye or a Color Applicator on the saber to select any of the original 16 blade colors.

## Data Sanctifier

<Row><ItemImage id="data_energistics:data_sanctifier" scale="4" /><RecipeFor id="data_energistics:data_sanctifier" /></Row>

Press use to throw the secondary immediately, then keep holding for 10 seconds and release to strike with the primary. Early release cancels the strike. Charging consumes 240 AE/s and stops when energy is insufficient. Life Reduction builds to level V: 5% less maximum health per level, up to 25%. It remains for 40 seconds after charging; expiration restores the limit without healing.

The secondary locks onto the creature it physically hits and embeds in its chest. Releasing a full charge blinks into a slash against that target within 8 blocks, without selecting another target in front. Players receive an execution and a 20-second spectator interval after respawning; non-player creatures take half panel damage. Execution protects creative players, spectators and operators, and respects player attack restrictions. If the target or blade moves beyond 8 blocks, the blade disappears and returns automatically. A blade that hits terrain remains there until approached. The first sanctifier player kill grants Strength III, Speed III, Resistance II and Data Overload IV for 20 seconds. Further sanctifier player kills during this period raise each effect one level and extend them by 3 seconds. Each Data Overload level adds 1% attack speed.

While a sanctifier is anywhere in the player's inventory, the curved primary blade rests diagonally across the back and the straight secondary hangs beside the left thigh. Selecting the sanctifier draws the primary from the back; switching away returns it behind the player. Quick switches continue from the current pose. The right hand grips the primary's handle with the blade held diagonally forward across the view, and ordinary attacks cycle through three slashes. Pressing use throws the secondary from the left hand immediately. The primary rises to the upper right and stays ready while charging, then blinks into a diagonal cut down to the left when a full charge is released.

Approach the blade or its target to recover it automatically. If the target dies, the blade stays at that location. The secondary disappears from the left-thigh mount while deployed. Charging is unavailable until recovery and resumes immediately afterward, with no cooldown. Neither blade uses a scabbard or emits sword waves.

## Data Trident

<Row><ItemImage id="data_energistics:data_trident" scale="4" /></Row>

Data Flow Storage research unlocks a 256-byte Data Flow capacity, refillable in the Data Charger and visible in its tooltip. Use still charges a throw; sneak-use switches repulsion and attraction. After Mob Field research, a flying or landed trident affects unobstructed non-player creatures within 4 blocks for 160 AE/s. It uses energy instead of durability.
