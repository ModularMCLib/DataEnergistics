## Modification Station Upgrades

Place the tool in a [Digital Transformation Research Station](6.22_digital_transformation_research_station.md) to research the energy capacity, speed, damage, mining speed, Data Flow storage, and abilities supported by that item. Cards are consumed as research materials. The Cell Workbench no longer installs cards, and old cards do not unlock research.

Energy research has three levels. Maximum energy is base capacity × (1 + 8 × energy level). Tools that support speed research have three speed levels. The cutting knife starts with 256 bytes of Data Flow storage and gains 512 bytes after research. Other tools unlock their Data Flow storage through a separate research node.

| Tool | Ability research |
| :--- | :--- |
| Crystal Sword | Fully charged attacks consume 20 Data Flow to stack Radix Redundancy |
| Crystal Axe | Chain felling for 20 Data Flow |
| Crystal Pickaxe | Ore duplication for 20 Data Flow |
| Crystal Hoe | Persistent moist farmland for 20 Data Flow |
| Crystal Shovel | Area mining for 20 Data Flow; sneak-use changes radius |
| Cutting Knife | Anchor selection expands from 9 to 11 chunks |
| Data Trident | Throws create a repulsion or attraction field with a 4-block radius for 160 AE/s |

Radix Redundancy only provides stacking bonus damage and the maximum-stack burst. Crystal swords, light sabers and rail Data Flow rounds apply only Redundancy. Radix Stasis is a separate effect that suspends creature AI, restores its original state afterward and does not restrict player input.

## Data Trident

<Row><ItemImage id="data_energistics:data_trident" scale="4" /></Row>

The Data Trident supports melee combat, throwing, and creature control. Base melee and thrown damage are both **17**, and its initial throw speed is **1.5 times** that of a vanilla trident. It runs on AE energy and has no durability. The bar below the item shows its current charge, and AE or FE equipment can recharge it.

### Using the trident

| Action | Effect and energy cost |
| --- | --- |
| Land a melee hit | Consumes 100 AE |
| Hold use, then release | Throws after at least 10 ticks (0.5 seconds) of charging; consumes 100 AE |
| Sneak-use while holding it | Switches between repulsion and attraction; repulsion is the default, and the tooltip shows the current mode |

Melee attacks and throws require at least 100 AE. Once thrown, the creature field draws from the trident's remaining charge.

### Modification Station research

The trident supports the following research, with upgrades stored on that individual item:

| Research | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Energy I / II / III | Raises maximum energy to 180,000 / 340,000 / 500,000 AE respectively; each level requires the previous one |
| Data Flow Storage | Unlocks 256 bytes of Data Flow storage, refillable in the [Data Charger](6.4_data_charger.md) and visible in the tooltip |
| Mob Field | Continuously repels or attracts nearby creatures while flying or landed, consuming 160 AE/s |

The field is centered on the thrown trident and affects non-player creatures within **4 blocks** that have an unobstructed path to it. Repulsion pushes them away, while attraction pulls them closer. After Mob Field research, a thrown trident consumes field energy even with no nearby targets. The field pauses when charge is insufficient; retrieve the trident to recharge it.

Mob Field is researched independently of Data Flow Storage and runs on the trident's AE charge. Energy research extends its operating time between charges.
