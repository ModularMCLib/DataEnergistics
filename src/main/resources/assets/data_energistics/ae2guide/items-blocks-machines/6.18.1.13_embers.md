# Embers

| Machine | Target | Admission and dispatch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alchemy Table | Alchemy Table | Pattern contains the center input, pedestal materials, and world-seed-selected aspects; the beam cannon is powered and has a clear line of sight. |

The adapter assigns pedestal materials and aspects, adjusts the beam cannon when needed, and invokes the native firing sequence. It collects the actual result, aspects, and native returns. Clear the center and pedestals before admission. Do not share inputs, outputs, or pipes with another task.
