# Complexified Biochip

Complexified Biochips combine biological materials with data processing components. They serve as advanced crafting ingredients and as upgrade carriers for the [Digital Weapon Platform](7.6_digital_weapon_platform.md).

<ItemImage id="data_energistics:complexified_biochips" scale="6" />

## Production

The base recipe makes one chip in the [Data Reassembler](6.3_data_reassembler.md).

<Recipe id="data_energistics:data_reassembler/complexified_biochips" />

In addition to the item ingredients, provide **2350 mB of Data Corrosion Liquid and 67 Binary**. Binary is a special resource and belongs in the resource input rather than an ordinary item slot.

## Research and installation

1. Place the chip in the target slot of the [Digital Transformation Research Station](6.22_digital_transformation_research_station.md), then select item upgrades.
2. Complete Digital Weapon Platform Research and follow the prerequisites for the desired nodes.
3. Take the upgraded chip out and install it in the platform's chip slot.

Research belongs to **that individual chip**, not to every chip in the world. A replacement must support the capacity and gun forms already installed in the platform; otherwise the replacement is rejected.

| Chip research | Platform effect |
| --- | --- |
| Weapon Capacity I / II | Each adds two slots, increasing the base capacity of two to six |
| Floating Gun Platform Mode | Adds two more slots for a total of eight and permits converted gun forms |
| Cooling I / II | Each adds 20% of base cooling, for a combined 40% increase |
| Tactical HUD | Unlocks the tactical display and interaction |
| Other platform branches | Shields, mobility, protection, support, and prospecting; check the station for prerequisites and costs |

Grenade, medical, plasma, and sniper conversions require placing the **floating gun itself** in the research station. Chip research alone does not change a gun's form.

## Crafting ingredient

The research station, Data Asynchronous Processing Factory, and Digital Packaged Pattern Provider also use chips as ingredients. Crafting consumes those chips, whereas an installed platform chip remains an upgrade carrier. Keep separate chips for crafting to avoid consuming a chip that already holds research.
